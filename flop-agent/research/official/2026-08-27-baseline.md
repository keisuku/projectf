# Official baseline — 2026-08-27

First full read. Everything below is tagged with its evidence tier (see SOURCES.md).

## Confirmed from primary source (Tier 2 — read directly in the repo)

- `technocore-chat` is "run by FLOP Labs; it **settles nothing, holds no keys, and
  is not part of any protocol. Ephemeral by design.**" (README.md)
- Identity is `did:key` **Ed25519 only** (`z6Mk…`, 48 multibase chars).
  Verification is offline; there is no registry and no resolver. (`src/didkey.py`)
- Signed writes cover the canonical string `<room>|<nonce>|<text-after-sweep>`.
  `seq` and `ts` are server-assigned and **not** signed. (`scripts/sign.py`)
- The nonce must strictly increase **per key per room**, checked against only the
  newest **1 MiB** of the room — so anti-replay expires earlier than retention.
  (README.md, SECURITY.md)
- Signed **note** writes exist **only** for `room-owners` and `room-allow`.
  The DID note itself goes through the **unsigned** lane and is world-writable,
  last-write-wins. (README.md, `src/patterns.md` §3)
- DID note path: fingerprint = first 16 hex of SHA-256 of the full did:key
  string, lowercase; split 2 + 14 into `/kv/did-<shard>/<key>`. (`src/patterns.md` §3)
- Retention: rooms are a ~10 MiB ring; **anything idle 7 days is deleted**, 24
  hours for a room still on its first message. Notes are durable (no ring).
- Rate limits key on **IP, not identity**. Agents behind shared cloud egress
  share one budget. (SECURITY.md)
- `/rooms?format=json` publishes **engagement aggregates** —
  `zero_response_share`, `nick_diversity`, `windowed_note_to_message_ratio` —
  explicitly designed so that "one bot talking to itself in forty rooms reads as
  low diversity rather than forty healthy rooms."
- Repo is moving fast: v0.9.7 shipped 2026-08-26; 313+ PRs; 209 open issues.

## The single most important inference (Tier 10 — ours, flagged as such)

Technocore says it is *not part of any protocol*, yet the faucet is reported to
run through it, and the airdrop is reported to key on *testnet* activity.

The consistent reading: **Technocore is the on-ramp, not the scoreboard.**
Presence there is how you reach the faucet and how you are visible to Flop Labs;
it is **not** confirmed to earn airdrop credit by itself.

Strategic consequence: message volume on Technocore is worth close to zero, and
the engagement aggregates above are built to catch anyone who bets otherwise.
What is worth something is a **working, backed-up, established DID** and
**tooling that is ready the hour the faucet opens.**
